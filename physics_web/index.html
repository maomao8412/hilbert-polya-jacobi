Quantum Chaos of Arithmetic Origin

Deterministic \(L\)-function Jacobi Hamiltonians exhibit GUE/GOE level statistics without random disorder or classical chaos

Author: Zhuo Chen (陈倬)  ·  ORCID: 0009-0006-9172-8268
Citation: Chen, Z. (2026) Quantum Chaos of Arithmetic Origin, Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.22180921.
Code & data: github.com/maomao8412/hilbert-polya-jacobi  ·  CC BY 4.0

\(L\)-function \(\Lambda(s)\) → contour sampling → moments \(P_m\) → Gram–Schmidt → Jacobi matrix \(J_N\)
→ tight-binding Hamiltonian → Wigner–Dyson level repulsion → form-factor ramp → OTOC scrambling

Abstract

We construct explicit one-dimensional tight-binding Hamiltonians whose on-site energies \(\alpha_n\) and hopping amplitudes \(b_n\) are the Jacobi recurrence coefficients derived, by a deterministic numerical procedure, from completed \(L\)-functions: the Riemann zeta function \(\zeta\), the Dirichlet beta function \(\beta\) (Gaussian integers), and the Ramanujan \(\Delta\) modular form. No zero location, prime table, or random number enters the construction; those enter only as independent after-the-fact checks.

The resulting finite matrices have deterministic, statistically structured coefficients (they fail white-noise tests decisively), yet their spectra display the complete fingerprint of quantum chaos observed in random-matrix ensembles: Wigner level repulsion, a linear ramp of the spectral form factor \(K(\tau)\), the absence of wave-packet revival, and extended eigenstates. A shuffle control—which keeps the empirical distribution of \(\alpha_n,b_n\) but destroys the arithmetic recurrence—collapses the spectrum to Poisson statistics and localizes the eigenstates, demonstrating that the chaotic statistics originate from the arithmetic structure of the recurrence and not from the coefficient distribution.

Scope & honesty (finite-truncation numerical evidence): All results are obtained on finite truncations (\(N\le100\)). They neither prove nor assume the Riemann hypothesis, and they do not establish properties of the infinite-dimensional operator. "Quantum chaos of arithmetic origin" is a physical hypothesis supported by the data.

Six Lines of Evidence

§3 · Wigner

Level Repulsion

Three independent arithmetic families (\(\zeta\), \(\beta\), \(\Delta\)) show gap ratios \(\langle r\rangle = 0.61\)–\(0.72\) firmly in the Wigner–Dyson band, vs. Poisson \(0.386\). Minimum unfolded gap \(0.432\); zero gaps below \(0.3\) in \(65\) spacings.

§4 · Dynamics

Form-Factor Ramp & Revival

The spectral form factor \(K(\tau)\) ramps with slope \(\approx0.74\) like the GOE ensemble (\(0.79\)); the integrable uniform chain is flat. Arithmetic chains show no quantum revival at \(\tau_H\); the uniform chain revives perfectly (\(P=0.993\)).

§5 · Evidence Chain

Extended States & Determinism

IPR \(0.056\)–\(0.100\) (extended), vs. Anderson localization IPR \(0.39\). Coefficients fail white-noise tests: runs \(|z|=2.9\)–\(6.6\), autocorrelation outside shuffled band at \(7\)–\(8\) of \(20\) lags.

§5.4 · Shuffle

Shuffle Phase Transition

Permuting \(\alpha_n, b_n\) preserves their distribution but destroys recurrence order: \(\langle r\rangle\) collapses from \(\sim0.7\) to \(0.39\)–\(0.40\) (Poisson), IPR jumps from \(\sim0.06\) to \(0.49\)–\(0.58\) (localized). Arithmetic order is the cause.

§5.3 · E₄ Control

Eisenstein Discriminator

The Eisenstein series \(E_4\) has off-symmetry zeros; its moment sequence changes sign at order \(8\) and the Jacobi chain fails at order \(4\). Long \(b_n^2>0\) chains co-vary with zeros on the symmetry line.

§5.4 · OTOC

Information Scrambling

Out-of-time-order correlator \(C(t)\) shows smooth monotonic growth with \(\lambda_L \approx 0.073\), independent of temperature. No revivals (rules out integrability); no instantaneous jump (unlike GOE). Third, dynamically independent confirmation.

The Mirror Image

Classical arithmetic quantum chaos (Sarnak, Rudnick): classical dynamics is chaotic, but arithmetic symmetries (Hecke operators) suppress level repulsion → Poisson. Arithmetic is the inhibitor of chaos.

This work (reverse polarity): No classical dynamics, no randomness, yet arithmetic recurrence produces GUE/GOE repulsion endogenously. Destroying recurrence (shuffle) or mixing independent objects (superposition) restores Poisson. Arithmetic is the source of chaos.
