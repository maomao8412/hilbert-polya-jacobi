Quantum Wave-Packet Spatiotemporal Evolution

Real-time evolution |ψ(t)⟩ = e−iJt|ψ(0)⟩ on three chains sharing identical coefficient distributions but different orderings: arithmetic (ζ-Jacobi), uniform crystal, and shuffled Anderson. The arithmetic chain exhibits a unique pseudo-Anderson regime — restricted spreading (RMS ≈ 10) without true localization.

Key insight: The quantum dynamics is controlled by the ordering of Jacobi coefficients, not their distribution. The arithmetic chain defines a new dynamical universality class — pseudo-Anderson localization with extended eigenstates and Wigner–Dyson spectral statistics.

§6 Experiment 4 — Interference Fingerprints ⭐

The most striking discovery: each L-function produces a unique quantum interference fingerprint \(F(s,t)\) when evolved under its Jacobi Hamiltonian. These fingerprints encode arithmetic information and show complete destructive interference (F ≈ −1) at the non-trivial zeros.

Key Observations

  1. Unique fingerprints: Each L-function produces a distinct F(s,t) pattern — the fingerprint encodes the arithmetic structure (Euler product, conductor, character).
  2. Complete destructive interference at zeros: At each non-trivial zero γₙ, F(s,t) drops to ≈ −1, indicating perfect phase cancellation — a direct quantum signature of the zeros.
  3. Arithmetic encoding: The three channels |Ch_alg|, |Ch_sing|, |Ch_reg| decompose the fingerprint into arithmetic (Euler product), singular (critical line pole structure), and regular contributions, revealing how different arithmetic features manifest quantum mechanically.
Connection to Hamiltonian: The fingerprint \(F(s,t) = \langle s | e^{-iHt} | s \rangle\) is the return amplitude of the Jacobi Hamiltonian. The fact that \(F \to -1\) at zeros means the time evolution produces perfect destructive interference precisely at the non-trivial zeros — establishing a direct bridge between quantum dynamics and the analytic structure of L-functions.

§7 Discussion & Three-Tier Claims

Concept 1

Arithmetic Quantum Chaos

The Jacobi matrices built from L-function data exhibit Wigner-Dyson statistics, wave-packet scrambling, and quantum-chaotic OTOC decay — despite having no classical chaotic dynamics. The chaos is of purely arithmetic origin.

Concept 2

Interference Fingerprints

Each L-function carries a unique quantum interference fingerprint. The zeros of the L-function manifest as points of complete destructive interference (F ≈ −1). This provides a physical observable that directly encodes analytic number theory.

Concept 3

Information Scrambling

OTOC decay with Lyapunov-like exponent λ_L < 0 (bounded by the system size) indicates information scrambling in the arithmetic Hilbert space — a purely quantum effect with no classical counterpart.
