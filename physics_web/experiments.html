§5.3 Eisenstein Series E₄ Discriminative Control

The Eisenstein series of weight 4 has \(L(E_4,s) = \zeta(s)\zeta(s-3)\) with elementary coefficients \(\sigma_3(n)\). Its completed function obeys \(\Lambda(s) = \Lambda(4-s)\) about \(s=2\), and its non-trivial zeros lie on \(\Re(s) = \frac{1}{2}\) and \(\Re(s) = \frac{7}{2}\) — offset from the symmetry center.

E₄ moment sequence P[m] — sign change at order 8
E₄ Jacobi chain: b²ₙ — fails at order 4

Result: Sampling \(\Xi_{E_4}(s) = s(s-4)\Lambda(s)\) through the identical pipeline (650-digit, 1536 contour points) reproduces \(\Xi_{E_4}(2) = \frac{1}{72}\) to ~15 digits, yet:

  • Moment sequence changes sign at order 8 (predicted by \(\cos(2m\theta_1)\) with \(\theta_1 \approx 0.106\))
  • Jacobi chain fails at order 4 (\(b_n^2\) non-positive; signs \(+ - - + - - + + - \cdots\))

Conclusion: Positive-definite moment matrices and long chains co-vary with zeros sitting on the symmetry line. The pipeline discriminates on-line from off-line zero distributions.

§5.4 Out-of-Time-Order Correlators (OTOC)

The squared commutator \(C(t) = \langle[W(t),V(0)]^\dagger[W(t),V(0)]\rangle_\beta\) with \(V = W = \hat{n}_{N/2}\). Tests whether the arithmetic chain exhibits information scrambling dynamics.
